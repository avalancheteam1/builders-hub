Deploying a contract costs a fee. On Fuji that fee is paid in test AVAX, which you can get for free.

## Why Anything Costs a Fee

Every transaction you send has to be executed by every validator on the network, and the result stored forever. The fee — usually called **gas** — pays for that work.

Gas also does something less obvious but more important: it makes spam expensive. If transactions were free, anyone could flood the network with junk. Attaching a small cost to every operation makes that pointless.

Deploying a contract costs more gas than a simple transfer, because the network is storing your contract's code permanently rather than just moving a number between two accounts. Even so, a token deployment on Fuji costs a small fraction of one AVAX.

Check your wallet. Within a few seconds your Fuji balance should change from `0` to something small, and the console header above will show the new balance too.

<Callout type="warn">
If the button says "Wait" with a countdown, this account has already claimed recently. At a live workshop, ask the host — they will usually have a backup way to fund you.
</Callout>
